I stirred awake

Something had caused that
my heart was lonely
It wished for a light
a light filled with promises and hope as dawn welcomes it on the horizon

It wished for a breeze
felt on my face on days when I did not have to put on a mask to stay safe

It wished for a kind word
when my thoughts sigh and need assurance, an unspoken promise as my hand is taken and peace radiated

Quiet down your manling heart
some dreams are not yours to dream and some words will never be whispered to your ears

You must endure alone
