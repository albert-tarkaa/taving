I live amongst the stars and draw breath each time a star dies
it's light, the last thing, maybe the only thing human eyes ever notice

To live your life on your own terms
yet out of reach
where the mortals can only imagine what you are and see you through glasses that hide your essence
To be wished on at your death when you are no more than a measure of time
