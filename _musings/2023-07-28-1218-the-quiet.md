It is all about the quiet, thickly woven around me and my thoughts

It is broken only by intrusions that seem like a stone, disturbing the smooth and utter quiet of a lake as it skids

I may have identified some piece of it, It is the quiet of a longing for things passed, buried and smothered in poorly patched pieces of my mind, the part that remembers what was and aches for it
