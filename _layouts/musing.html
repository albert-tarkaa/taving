Liquid has no index-of, and a negative index would wrap to the end of the array, hence the explicit guards. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ordered = site.musings | sort: 'date' -%} {%- assign here = 0 -%} {%- for m in ordered -%} {%- if m.url == page.url -%}{%- assign here = forloop.index0 -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign last = ordered.size | minus: 1 -%} {%- if here > 0 -%} {%- assign back = here | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign prev = ordered[back] -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if here < last -%} {%- assign ahead = here | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign next = ordered[ahead] -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign words = content | number_of_words -%} {%- assign reading = words | plus: 100 | divided_by: 200 -%} {%- if reading < 1 -%}{%- assign reading = 1 -%}{%- endif -%}
